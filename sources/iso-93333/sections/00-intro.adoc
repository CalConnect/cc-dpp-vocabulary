== Introduction

A digital product passport makes product-related information available to economic operators, authorities and other parties over the life of a product. The same designations are used today with different definitions in different instruments. Without a single vocabulary, systems that claim conformance to those instruments do not interoperate on meaning.

This document is that vocabulary. It defines the terms required for digital product passport work in ISO/IEC JTC 5 and for referring documents.

Two classes of entry are distinguished. *Structural terms* name the speech acts, roles and mechanisms of the passport framework — attestation, classification, designation, profile, custody transfer — and are defined globally. *Domain-scoped entries* name concepts whose criteria of application are owned by a community: jurisdictions (placed on the market), sectors (textile product), or cultural and appraisal communities (quality designations). Domain-scoped entries are recorded for navigational completeness; each carries a NOTE to that effect, and nothing in this document harmonizes their criteria across communities. Where correspondence between communities exists, it is expressed as a registered mapping item in a semantic registry maintained under the disciplines of ISO 19135, not by definition. Interoperability in this framework is a matter of shared negotiability — identity, time, attestation form, measurement discipline and trust — rather than of shared meaning.

Clause 3 is organized by subject: the digital product passport itself; identity and carriers; actors; life cycle; trust and verification; semantics and profiles; measurement; and the concepts of projection, provenance, composition, capability and federation used when a passport is operated across jurisdictions and systems. Where a definition is taken from another International Standard, that standard is indicated with the entry.
