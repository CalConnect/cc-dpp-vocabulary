=== Conflicting definitions reproduced in full

The following subclauses reproduce, for each designation whose published definitions differ across sources, the verbatim definition text of each cited source, and the clause number from which it is taken. Where this document uses one definition in Clause 3, the adopted text is reproduced under "Definition adopted". Other published texts are reproduced under "Other published definitions". The substantive variance is described under "Variance".

==== digital product passport (Clause 3.1.1)

*Definition adopted*

ISO/AWI 25534-1, 3.1::

  A concept or technology which enables sharing of product related information that is essential for product sustainability and circularity, including those specified in regulations, across all relevant economic actors.

*Other published definitions*

EN 18223:2026, 3.4::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle

EN 18216:2026, 3.7::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its lifecycle

EN 18221:2026, 3.2::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle

EN 18222:2026, 3.2::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle

EN 18239:2026, 3.9::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle

EN 18246:2026, 3.2::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle

EN 18219:2026, 3.1.3::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle that is accessible via electronic means through a data carrier

EN 18220:2026, 3.6::

  digital record of product characteristics throughout its life cycle that is accessible via electronic means through a data carrier

NOTE: Example characteristics include environmental sustainability, environmental impact, and recyclability. (EN 18216:2026, 3.7; EN 18221:2026, 3.2; EN 18222:2026, 3.2; EN 18223:2026, 3.4; EN 18239:2026, 3.9; EN 18246:2026, 3.2; EN 18219:2026 and EN 18220:2026 carry the same note text.)

*Variance.* ISO/AWI 25534-1 names the subject as a *concept or technology* that *enables sharing of product-related information* across relevant economic actors. EN 18216:2026 to EN 18246:2026 name the subject as a *digital record of product characteristics* throughout the product's *life cycle*. EN 18219:2026 and EN 18220:2026 add the qualifier "accessible via electronic means through a data carrier".

==== unique identifier (Clause 3.2.2)

*Definition adopted*

ISO 29404:2015, 3.26 (cited by CWA 18291:2025, 3.3)::

  identifier which is guaranteed to be unique among all identifiers used for those objects and for a specific purpose

NOTE: For more information, see ISO/IEC 15459-1. (CWA 18291:2025, 3.3 note.)

*Other published definitions*

EN 18219:2026, 3.1.24::

  string of characters that is required to be unique among all identifiers used for all objects for a specific purpose

NOTE: A unique identifier refers to unique product identifier (3.1.25), unique economic operator identifier (3.1.22) and unique facility identifier (3.1.23).

EN 18220:2026, 3.18::

  string of characters that is required to be unique among all identifiers used for all objects for a specific purpose

*Variance.* EN 18219:2026 and EN 18220:2026 formulate the entry in terms of a *string of characters* required to be unique among *all* identifiers for *all objects*. ISO 29404:2015 / CWA 18291:2025 / ISO/IEC 15459 family formulate the entry in terms of an *identifier* guaranteed to be unique among identifiers for *those objects* and a specific purpose. EN 18219:2026 modifies ISO 29404:2015 as follows: "identifier" is replaced by "string of characters", "those objects" is replaced by "all objects", and the original Note 1 to entry is replaced by a new Note 1 listing the three identifier types.

==== economic operator (Clause 3.3.1)

*Definition adopted*

EN 18221:2026, 3.1::

  manufacturer, authorized representative, importer, distributor, dealer or fulfilment service provider

*Other published definitions*

EN 18219:2026, 3.1.5::

  manufacturer, authorized representative, importer, distributor, dealer or fulfilment service provider that places the product on the market

EN 18220:2026, 3.7::

  manufacturer, authorized representative, importer, distributor, dealer or fulfilment service provider that places the product onto the market

*Variance.* EN 18219:2026 and EN 18220:2026 include the qualifier "that places the product on the market" / "onto the market"; EN 18221:2026 does not.

==== interoperability (Clause 3.6.1)

*Definition adopted*

ISO/IEC 22123-1:2023, 3.6.1 (cited by EN 18219:2026, 3.1.7)::

  ability of two or more systems or applications to exchange information and to mutually use the information that has been exchanged

*Other published definitions*

EN 18223:2026, 3.1 (lineage: ISO 18435-1:2009, 3.12)::

  capability of two or more entities to exchange items in accordance with a set of rules and mechanisms implemented by an interface in each entity, in order to perform their specified tasks

*Variance.* The two formulations derive from different parent vocabularies. ISO/IEC 22123-1:2023 (cloud computing) speaks of *systems or applications* that *exchange information* and *mutually use* it. ISO 18435-1:2009 (industrial automation diagnostics integration) speaks of *entities* that *exchange items* through *an interface implementing a set of rules and mechanisms*.
